JudgeGPT

The game where AI decides you or your friends' fate in the courtroom. Efficient and succinct justice, with all the bias and inaccuracy of AI.

No longer running · 2023–2024

How it played

  • A futuristic small-claims court where AI has replaced the lawyers and the judge.
  • The AI decided what you were accused of. Then you got one chance to prove your innocence.
  • One live courtroom for everyone. If you weren't in the hearing you were in the audience, chatting and waiting your turn.
  • The judge, the verdicts and the drama were all voiced by AI.
