Care Givers

Caregiver training at an older patient's bedside.

2020 · Stage at last update: Prototype · With The Ageing Revolution

Aged careHealthcare

A prototype on caregiver training and discrimination faced by people of colour, set in a hospital room with an older patient. Carers wash their hands at the sink and take a blood pressure reading with a manual machine.

The prototype was built to support further funding for a full program.
