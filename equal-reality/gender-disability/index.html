Gender & Disability

Equal Reality's first and most-run experience.

2017 · Funded by Remarkable, Telstra Foundation, NSW Family & Community Services and Cerebral Palsy Alliance

ViveRiftGenderDisability

Released for the HTC Vive in 2017, Gender & Disability became the most popular VR app in Australia on Viveport and the most popular business app globally. Remastered for the Oculus Rift in 2018, it has been run on every continent except Antarctica.

The experience

Step into the shoes of AllieHead of marketing, navigating gender discrimination in a conversation with your manager and a client.
Step into the shoes of MattA web developer who uses a wheelchair, patronized in a workplace conversation while a colleague brushes past it.

Key biases

  • Patronizing language
  • Assumption of role
  • Age bias
  • Exclusionary behaviour
  • Idea poaching
