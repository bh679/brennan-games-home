In Groups & Out Groups

Left out of the loop, then dismissed for asking to be included.

2019 · With Carlton United Breweries, part of the award-winning program We're All In

InclusionGender

The second VR application in Carlton United Breweries' We're All In program. It has three parts: an empathy experience, a data scene with real-time feedback on bias identification, and a reflection scene.

The experience

You are a brewing managerIn the office kitchenette you learn critical decisions were made outside work hours. Ask to be included and receive biased feedback in return.

Key biases

  • Decisions made outside business hours
  • In-group / out-group
  • Gender bias
