Just Another Domestic

Respond to a domestic violence call as a NSW Police officer.

2019 · Built with NSW Police

PoliceSituational awareness

A VR training simulation that puts you in the shoes of a NSW Police officer conducting a domestic violence investigation, with decisions centred on procedure and officer safety.

Like pilots in a flight simulator, officers live through the call and can fail or succeed in a risk-free space, instead of imagining it from a lecture or role play.

Covers

  • Unconscious bias training
  • Situational awareness
  • Procedure and officer safety
