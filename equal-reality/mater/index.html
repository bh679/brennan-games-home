Speaking with Good Judgement

Hospital staff rehearse speaking up, inside the rooms they'll have the conversation in.

With Mater Education, Mater Foundation and Advance Queensland; co-designed with Harvard's Center for Medical Simulation

HealthcareSpeaking upPico

Speaking with Good Judgement is Mater's own program for giving feedback and speaking up respectfully, the heart of a psychologically safe hospital. Face-to-face participation was waning, so Mater asked for a VR version: Through the Looking Glass.

Staff stand in recreations of Mater's own wards and break rooms. A narrator guides them as animated colleagues work through difficult feedback, they choose at each branch, and they see how each communication style plays out.

Reach

  • Delivered face to face to more than 60 staff across Brisbane and regional campuses
  • About 100 participants across five Queensland locations in the pilot, on Pico Neo 3 headsets
  • Mater's own facilitators trained to keep running it in-house
