Live My Experience

Disability awareness at the beach and the shopping centre.

With Central Coast Council NSW and its Access and Inclusion Reference Group, and People Tech Revolution

QuestDisabilityLocal government

Part of Central Coast Council's strategy to improve disability access and inclusion. Instead of a checklist of ramps, it lets community members, businesses and council staff feel, briefly and safely, what everyday barriers are like from the inside.

A first-person experience set in two places most people never think twice about: a beach and a shopping centre. Delivered more than 200 times, it is used in teacher training, council staff training and CSIRO-facilitated sessions.

From exclusion to inclusion

  • Exclusion: being prevented or denied access because of difference
  • Segregation: needs are catered for, but separately from everyone else
  • Integration: placed in the mainstream, but expected to adapt to it
  • Inclusion: the environment is designed so everyone can take part
“The experience was an incredible eye opener into the difficulties faced by people with a disability to engage in everyday activities.”Participant
