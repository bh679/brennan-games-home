Think Ahead

Culturally competent advance care planning and end-of-life conversations.

With the Multicultural Communities Council of the Illawarra (MCCI) and The Ageing Revolution

Aged careCultureHealth

MCCI leads Partners in Culturally Appropriate Care in NSW and the ACT, helping aged care providers give culturally appropriate care to older people from culturally and linguistically diverse (CALD) communities.

Think Ahead is two apps built from real-life scenarios gathered through facilitated feedback. They deepen understanding of experiences different from your own and model better communication in palliative care.
