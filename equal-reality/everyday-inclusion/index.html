Everyday Inclusion

A three-part interactive journey through unconscious bias, microaggressions and intersectionality.

2020 · Co-designed with Cornell University's Diversity & Inclusion team and Interactive Theater

QuestUnconscious biasRaceGender

Everyday Inclusion is a three-part VR blended-learning course on unconscious bias, inclusion, microaggressions and intersectionality, including themes of race and gender.

Each part takes about 20 minutes: learn a little theory, live through a workplace meeting where bias plays out, reflect on what happened, then commit to strategies for interrupting bias.

The experience

Step into the shoes of Tamara, the new hireAn organizational design specialist four months into a new job. Everyone is nice, mostly. But small things, subtle and not so subtle, keep happening.
