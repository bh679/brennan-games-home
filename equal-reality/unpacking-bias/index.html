Unpacking Bias

Immersive bias and allyship training, delivered remotely during COVID.

2020 · With ViacomCBS

WebRemoteAllyship

Inspired by Black Lives Matter, ViacomCBS wanted employees to understand experiences different from their own and learn how to be an ally.

With COVID ruling out in-person sessions and shared headsets, Unpacking Bias became web-based simulations blended with facilitator-led Zoom workshops, co-designed with employees from real lived experiences. It launched as part of ViacomCBS's Inclusion Week.
