Speak to Me Normally

Social anxiety at work, felt from the inside.

Designed from lived experience

Mental healthAnxiety

Most people know a colleague who goes quiet in meetings. Few know what the room feels like from that seat. Speak to Me Normally puts you in it, first person, with the person opposite close enough to read and no way to put off the answer.

Afterwards the team debriefs and agrees how it will speak, ask and wait for each other.
