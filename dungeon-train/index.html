Dungeon Train

A free Minecraft mod by Brennan Hatton. Ride an endless train through procedurally generated dungeons. 《地牢列车》— 由 Brennan Hatton 开发的免费 Minecraft 模组。

What Dungeon Train is

Dungeon Train is a mod for Minecraft: Java Edition, built for NeoForge 1.21.1. It adds a moving train that hosts procedurally generated dungeons — you fight your way carriage to carriage as the train rolls on. It is a Minecraft port of the original Dungeon Train game.

The mod is free to download and free to play. It is published on the two standard Minecraft mod platforms, where you can see its release history, download counts, and player reviews:

Support development

Dungeon Train is developed by one person and given away for free. Players around the world have asked for a way to contribute towards its continued development, so this page sets out how that works and what it does and does not mean.

《地牢列车》由一人开发并免费提供。本页面说明玩家如何自愿支持它的持续开发。

What contributions pay for

  • Ongoing development time — new content, bug fixes, and Minecraft version updates
  • Server and hosting costs for the mod's online features
  • Art, sound, and other assets

What contributions are not

Nothing is being sold. A contribution does not buy the mod, unlock content, grant in-game items or advantages, or provide any goods or services in return. The mod is free to everyone, whether or not they contribute, and always will be. Contributions are entirely voluntary.

本页面不出售任何商品。捐助不会解锁内容、不会提供游戏内物品或优势。 无论是否捐助，本模组对所有玩家始终免费。捐助完全出于自愿。

Contributions & refunds

Contributions are voluntary and non-refundable, as no goods or services are provided in exchange. If a payment was made in error, is duplicated, or you believe your payment method was used without your permission, contact Brennan@brennanhatton.com and it will be refunded.

捐助为自愿性质，原则上不予退款，因为并未提供任何商品或服务作为交换。 如果付款有误、重复扣款，或您认为付款方式被未经授权使用，请联系 Brennan@brennanhatton.com，我们将予以退款。

Revolut, Alipay, and WeChat Pay contributions are one-off payments with no recurring charge. Patreon supporters may choose a monthly pledge, which can be cancelled at any time from your Patreon account. Payments are handled by the provider you choose — Patreon, Revolut, or Stripe — and your card or wallet details are never seen or stored by Brennan.Games.

Privacy

Brennan.Games does not collect personal information from this page. If you contribute, your chosen provider (Patreon, Revolut, or Stripe) processes the payment and shares only what is needed to confirm it, such as the amount, currency, and payment method type. If you email support, your message and address are used only to answer you. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.
